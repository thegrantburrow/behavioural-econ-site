Articles get lost inside sections

Reading the Research alone has 24 essays. Once you open one, the rest mostly live in a nav dropdown. Three ways to keep the article findable without turning every page into a directory.

Pick a direction. Toggle Looks good / Needs work on each option, add notes, then copy feedback. These are IA/chrome options for the live site, not copy edits.
Option A · Chrome on every article

You-are-here trail + sibling strip

Put orientation on the article itself: a breadcrumb back to the section hub, plus a horizontal strip of other essays in that section with the current one marked. Recovery without opening the nav, and without dumping a full directory into the body.

Tapping the section name returns to the hub. Swiping the strip browses siblings. Same pattern could apply to Principles categories, Science Behind domains, and Experiments.

Option B · Full list, one click away

Collapsible section index on every article

A closed-by-default panel near the top: “All 24 essays in Reading the Research.” Open it and you get the full section list, current piece highlighted. Solves the buried-nav problem without permanent chrome eating the first viewport.

Option C · One map, articles point back

Make the section hub the map; articles only point back

Don't put 24 links on every article. Upgrade each section hub into a scannable, filterable index of everything in that section. On the article: a clear breadcrumb, a single “Browse all essays in this section” button, and the existing short Continue reading pair. The hub is where you recover; the article stays an article.
