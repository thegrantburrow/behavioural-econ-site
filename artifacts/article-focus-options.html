One article, or the whole library?

Principles, Field Sessions, Experiments, and Science Behind each live in one long file. A link to Anchoring opens that section, but the page is still the hub: category tiles above, the other articles above and below. Special reports already work the other way. Each one is its own page.

The decision. When someone opens one article from search, a share link, or a cross-link, should that article be its own page, or should the long file stay and the landed article be the thing you actually see?
Option A · Split the files

One file per article

Do what the special reports already do. Anchoring becomes its own page. The Principles page stays an index of links. Opening Anchoring never shows the other 82, because they are not on that page.

Option B · Focus the link

Same file. The link shows only that article.

Keep principles.html as one file. If the address is #anchoring, hide the hub and every other principle. A bar names the article and the way back. The library is still one click away. It is not the page you landed on.
