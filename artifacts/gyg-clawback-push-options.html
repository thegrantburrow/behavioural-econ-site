List bridge

Hossain & List (Management Science 2012) paid the same RMB bonus either as “earn it if you hit target” or “it’s already yours; we take it back if you miss.” Teams produced about 1% more under the loss frame. GYG is doing the consumer version: a reward already credited in language, with expiry as the clawback.

Apply (loyalty / app)

Credit the reward in copy before the deadline, name what lapses, leave a real window to act, and keep the channel honest. Pair loss framing with a disclosed expiry date, the way Up’s bonus-interest nudge does for banking, not a surprise forfeiture after the fact.

Cite: Hossain & List 2012; Kahneman & Tversky, Econometrica 1979 (reference points); site principles above. Photo is GYG’s own marketing text, not proof of incremental redemptions.

List tie-in (one paragraph in article)

Fryer, Levitt, List & Sadoff (AEJ: Economic Policy 2022) ran the same teacher bonus as end-of-year gain vs up-front payment clawed back for missed targets. Pooled loss frame ≈ 0.124σ on math scores vs ≈ 0.051σ for gain frame. Mention as “clawback at scale outside the factory,” with the usual caveat that wave two faded.

Reading the Researchreport

Clawback / loss-framed incentives

When “already yours, don’t lose it” works, and when it backfires

0Reference points. Kahneman & Tversky (1979): value is judged on gains and losses from a reference point; losses bite harder. Clawback engineering moves the reference point up before the deadline.
1Factory (+). Hossain & List (2012): prepaid bonus clawed back for missed output ≈ +1% team productivity vs gain-framed bonus.
2Schools (+/−). Fryer, Levitt, List & Sadoff (2022): loss-framed teacher pay beat gain-framed in pooled math scores; second wave null, so treat persistence as open.
3Dealerships (−). Pierce, Rees-Jones & Blank (2020): prepaid sales bonuses clawed back cut sales ≈ 5% at 294 dealerships (gaming and effort reallocation), a real counterweight to “always use loss framing.”

Worked example

GYG GOMEX push: headline = protect; body = endowed free item + dated expiry + app channel. Same structure as factory clawback, different stakes (fries, not wages) and no published A/B on the headline.

Field guide

(1) Only use loss framing when the reward is real, dated, and redeemable as described. (2) Test gain vs loss copy where you can; List’s own portfolio includes wins and nulls. (3) Watch for gaming when clawback touches sales targets or multi-step redemption. (4) Keep Up-style warnings as the banking benchmark for “honest clawback.”
