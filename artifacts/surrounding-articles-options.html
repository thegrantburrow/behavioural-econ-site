How should the articles around it look?

Not the menu. On the page itself, after an anchor lands on one article: what should the other articles around it look like, so it does not feel like you dropped into the whole library?

Pick a treatment for the surrounding pieces. The landed article stays open and highlighted in every option. What changes is everything else on that same long page.
Option A · Keep them in the scroll

Ghost title rows, still in place

Every other article stays where it is in the page, but collapses to a muted one-line title. You can still scroll past dozens of ghosts. The library is visible as a dim list wrapping the piece you opened.

Option B · One list, not a stack of ghosts

Scoop the others into one drawer

On an anchor land, the other articles leave the main column. They become one compact “Also in Principles (82)” drawer above or below the focused piece. The page body is mostly just the article you opened.

Option C · Thin context, hide the rest

Only the near neighbours stay visible
