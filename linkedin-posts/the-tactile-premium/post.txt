Why does NRMA still print and post a magazine that's been running since 1927, to over 1.3 million members, when the same content already sits in their own app for free?

Was genuinely curious about this after Open Road turned up in my own letterbox, well over a year after NRMA launched a full digital hub alongside it. Wrote up the actual research behind why a physical copy can outperform a free digital one.

A small sample of what it's built on:

🖐️ Touching an object, even briefly, makes people feel like they already own it, and pay more for it
📄 72 students scored significantly higher reading the same text on paper than on an identical screen
💰 Across five separate experiments, people valued and paid more for a physical version of identical content

The part that actually made me write this up: a newer study from two of the same researchers behind the touch finding. It found psychological ownership of something physical predicts hotel guest loyalty, independent of how satisfied people are with the stay itself.

For anyone weighing acquisition and retention, that's a lever satisfaction scores alone would miss.

Digital is still cheaper to produce and send. Touch, memory, and price all point the other way.

Full piece, with all the citations: https://www.grantburrow.com/the-tactile-premium.html
