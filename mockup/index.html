Zero Price Effect

A price drops to free. Demand doesn't rise a little. It jumps, even when the discount to get there was identical.

Goal Gradient Effect

A loyalty card arrives with two stamps already on it. You finish it faster, for the exact same reward.

Default Effect

Whatever's pre-selected gets chosen, far more often than the exact same option would if you had to pick it.
