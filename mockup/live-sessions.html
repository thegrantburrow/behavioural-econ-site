    Live Sessions

    Behavioural science, run live in the room

    Everything else on this site is something to read. This is something to do. Scan a QR code at a real talk, take part in a real behavioural economics demonstration alongside everyone else in the room, and watch the results appear as the room answers. First entry: Anchoring, more sessions are planned to follow the same real-research standard as everywhere else on this site.

    Pilot session

    Anchoring, Live

    Two halves of the room see the same question, seeded with two different numbers. Neither side knows it.

    Likely mechanismThe first number you see anchors your guess, even when you know it's arbitrary

    This runs the same paradigm Tversky and Kahneman used in their original 1974 anchoring study: a comparative judgment against a planted number, then a free estimate of the real answer. Half the room is asked whether the real figure is more or less than a high number; the other half, the same question against a low number. Nobody is told which anchor they got, or that the room split at all. Then everyone guesses the same real fact, and the two groups' answers get compared live.

    Try it: Group A Try it: Group B Watch live results

    Browsing on your own? Pick one link and don't peek at the other before you answer, that's the whole demonstration. The tool opens on claude.ai, outside this site, since that's what makes the live results actually live.

    How this runs at a live talk

    1. Two QR codes go up around the room, printed identically apart from which one a seat happens to be near. Neither is labelled “high” or “low.”
    2. Everyone scans, answers the comparative question, then estimates the real fact. The whole thing takes under a minute.
    3. The presenter pulls up the reveal screen once enough answers are in: both groups' medians, side by side, with the real answer marked.
    4. The gap is the lesson. Same room, same real fact, same instructions, two different numbers because of a planted anchor nobody agreed to.
    See the full mechanism, citation, and methodology critique: Anchoring.
    More sessions like this are planned. Anchoring is the pilot for this category; the same real-research standard (a genuine published paradigm, not a party trick) will apply to whatever comes next.
